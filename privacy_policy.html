Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Breezo handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Breezo may access your device's location only with your permission. Location information is used solely to provide local weather forecasts and weather-related information.

How We Use Information

We use location data to:

  • Determine your current location.
  • Retrieve weather information relevant to your area.
  • Improve the accuracy of weather forecasts displayed in the app.

Third-Party Services

The app uses our partnered API to provide weather data. Weather information is obtained from this API and is subject to their applicable terms and policies.

Data Storage

Breezo does not sell your personal information. Location data is used only to provide weather services.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
